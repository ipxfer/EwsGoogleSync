EwsGoogleSync

EwsGoogleSync is a small personal script that runs on its author's own computer. It reads the author's meetings from a work Exchange calendar and copies them into a calendar in the author's own Google account.

It is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, there is nothing to sign up for, and it does not access anyone else's Google account.

Read the privacy policy for how it handles data.
